# Dassie's visual identity is Kopje: solid rocks, nimble dassies, first light

**Areas:** [Website](../areas/website.md).

## Facts

- Dassie is a payment network that its users run for each other. It wants to look approachable, positive and communal, the opposite of the tall, imposing building a bank stands for, and still be credible with money: never silly, never the neon-on-black look of crypto projects.
- The name and the mascot are the dassie, the rock hyrax: a social animal that lives in colonies on kopjes, the low rocky outcrops of the southern African veld. The project has hand-drawn dassie illustrations in ink line art, with a violet cube for value.
- Dassie settles on ledgers that already exist (the XRP Ledger today), and its nodes route payments between each other: the ledgers are the part that holds still, the nodes the part that moves.
- A mark has to hold at 16 px in a browser tab and on light and dark grounds, and a site, a wallet and things printed for operators all have to come from one set of rules.

## Decision

- **Kopje.** The ledgers are solid rocks; the nodes are nimble dassies living on them, hopping from rock to rock. Everything lies low and wide, lit from behind by a low sun. The brand page, `apps/website/brand/`, holds the identity, its rules and its applications, and is its only source.
- **The mark, Perch:** a dassie sitting on a faceted rock in front of the sun. The rock is ink like the dassie, parted from it by a rim of light; below 48 px the Silhouette, dassie and rock as one shape in the sun's circle, takes its place.
- **Type and color:** Fraunces for display and the wordmark, Figtree for text, DM Mono for data, served by the site itself. Sandstone grounds, Sunrise light, granite for the kopjes in scenes and Terracotta for the earth beneath them ([granite-rocks](granite-rocks.md)), Ember for action, Ink for text, and violet only for value: the cube.
- **The line:** "Any currency. Any ledger. Anyone." It says payments, says what is different, and ends on people. The tagline beside it is "Banks build up. We build out."
- **Words** in plain American English.
- **Dark mode is dusk:** what is ink melts into the night and only rims of light stay. Product surfaces switch; specimens and printed things keep their colors.

## Accepted

- The drawings are heavy: grass is drawn blade by blade, a few megabytes of SVG in all. They are files beside the page, so the page itself stays short enough to read; the dark mode recolors only the drawings inline in it.
- The mark's rock is ink rather than terracotta. Brown read as something else to people who saw the mark; ink costs the mark its third color, and the kopjes in scenes are granite ([granite-rocks](granite-rocks.md)).
- In dark mode the drawings have little contrast by design: silhouettes against the night, carried by their rims of light.
- Terracotta is one color, the darkest earth, so that a card of earth stands apart from Ember, which is action, and carries Daylight text at any size. At dusk its small marks, a trust bar or a bullet on a night surface, take a lighter step of it, as dark as that they would not show. The balance card and the veld grass are darker for it, and the grass reads there by the sunlit edges of its blades.
- The wallet and the desktop app keep their look until they take Kopje ([roadmap](../roadmap.md#kopje-across-dassie)).

## Rejected

- *Huddle,* dassies piled into one warm heap, in bright flat colors: communal, but its picture is about the animals keeping warm, says nothing of ledgers or routes, and a heap reads as one lump rather than a network of nodes that each stand on their own.
- *Constellation,* every node a star in a dusk sky: it shows the network best, but a night sky with glowing nodes sits close to the look of crypto projects that Dassie must not have.
- *Field Notes,* an expedition's log book: it holds the long effort and the fun of building, but it is about the builders' journey rather than what someone using Dassie gets, and its paper and ink are weak on a wallet's screens.
- *Neighbourhood Press,* a street's own newsletter in bright off-register inks: lively and communal, but a zine reads as casual where Dassie has to be trusted with money, and does not carry over to a wallet.
- *A lighter Terracotta:* a clay one sits between Ember and Sunrise and carries small Daylight text too faintly, and a middle one is as light as Ember, so a card of earth and the button on it are told apart only by hue.
- *"Low and wide" as the line:* it described the look, not the product; on a tote it said nothing about payments.
