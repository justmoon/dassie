Kopje

Banks build up. We build out.

A kopje is a low rocky outcrop in the African veld. The rocks are solid and stable; on top lives a colony of nimble dassies, hopping from rock to rock, with the rocks as their safety and their home. Every morning they climb out together into the first sun.

Any currency. Any ledger. Anyone. A payment network its users run themselves. Sandstone · Sunrise · Granite · Ember · Violet Crystal
2The idea

Solid rocks, nimble dassies.

Dassie is a kopje: low, wide, and open to anyone who climbs on. Everything in Kopje is low and wide.

The rocks are the ledgers: the XRP Ledger, Bitcoin. Solid, stable and dependable. On top lives a society of nimble dassies, jumping from rock to rock, with the rocks as their safety and their home.

Each node is a dassie. It runs on its owner's own server, peers with a few neighbors, keeps its own books and protects itself. Nobody sits on top of anybody.

The rocks do the holding. Payments hop from node to node; settlement lands on a ledger that does not move. The stability comes from the rock, not from a tall building.

Trust grows sideways. Each node sends more of its payments through the peers that have earned it the most. Good neighbors get busier.

Sunrise is the mood. The colony climbs out together into the first light: a new day, warm and hopeful. It is also honest. Dassie is early, but the sun is coming up.

So the identity lies down: horizons, kopjes, wide compositions and warm light, with the sun behind everything. Nothing towers. Nothing glows neon. The only thing that shines is the violet cube, because that is the value moving between friends.

3Logo system

One rock, one dassie, one sun.

The mark is Perch: a dassie sitting on a faceted rock with the sun rising behind it. The rock is lit from behind, so only its top edge catches the light, and the dassie sits on that lit edge. Its small cut, the Silhouette, is the same drawing seen through the sun's circle, all in ink. The wordmark is “Dassie” in soft Fraunces, with a capital D: it is a name, and Fraunces’ round D is as friendly as the rest. The mark as files: Sunrise and the Silhouette, in SVG.

On Ink
Sunrise down to 48 px, the Silhouette below it
  • One axis. The sun is centered on the mark; the rock is barely wider than the sun, so the mark is nearly square.
  • A faceted rock. Flat faces and tight corners: rock, not dough. Its top face rises 6° to the right, and the dassie is drawn to sit on that slope.
  • Backlit. The rock is ink, the dassie's own color, its body turned away from the sun. Only its top faces catch the light, a rim of Sunrise. The dassie sits on that rim, so a line of light separates it from the rock.
  • No face. The dassie is a pure silhouette: the round back, the blunt muzzle, the small ear.
  • One shape in the Silhouette. Dassie and rock are a single ink path, cut to the sun's circle, so no seam can show.
  • Clear space is half the sun's radius on every side. In the lockup the rock's base sits on the wordmark's baseline.

4Color

Warm stone, first light, and one violet thing that matters.

6Graphic language

Horizons, backlit rock and first light.

Six elements, all lying down, all lit from behind. They carry the brand when there is no illustration, and they give the illustrations ground to stand on.

Kopje line

Ends a page or a major section, and carries the footer: an ink horizon with kopjes of granite blocks, lit from behind, now and then a dassie drawn for its rock.

Aa

Rim light

The sun is behind, so light shows as a rim on top edges. Every divider is 2 px ink under 2 px Sunrise. On a headline (0.045 em) or a dark card (2.5 px), once or twice per site.

Veld grass

Bunch grass, grown in code and never stamped: clumps of every size, blades that arch under their weight and catch the sun along their outer half, rooigras nodding above. At the dassies' scale. On the balance card and in the footer.

Big sun

One flat disc, no glow and no rings, for a moment: a hero, a release, a node coming up. One per screen, never with the mark inside it. A dassie in front of it sits well inside it or clear of it, never on its edge.

Hop trail

Faint dotted hops in warm stone, only where something moves: a payment's route, setup progress. Nothing rides on them.

Node circles

Every node's avatar: the sun with its own granite rock, drawn from its node ID, so a node looks the same in peer lists, routes and maps.

Rocks: granite, never brown

A shape reads as droppings when four things come together: a dark warm brown, a soft rounded outline, one lump with nothing inside to say what it is, and the size of the animal beside it. A rock in Kopje has none of them.

Granite, lit from behind. Faces are Granite, the ends Granite Light, where the light wraps around. The shade is Granite Shade, low on the face: its top follows the block's top edge, flat across the middle and falling away toward the ends, a little uneven, its weight away from the sun. Far kopjes and node circles are one tone. Never Terracotta, which is earth.
Flat faces, cut corners. Straight edges with their corners cut short, flat or tilted tops, never a dome. A crack across a face shows the joints.
Stacked. A near kopje is two or more blocks, a broad base and smaller ones on it. A far one, too small for its parts, is one low, wide slab.
Bigger than the dassies. A dassie's rock is at least three times its length. One lying down lies toward an end, never on the crown of a small rock.
Lit by the sun. The rim is Sunlit Granite: widest near the sun and on edges facing it, tapering down the sides, never on an edge facing down. A block resting on another lies behind its top edge, which is lit at the block's sides, tapers to a point and stays dark in the middle; a line of shade lies at the block's foot.
Framed or clear. The sun holds a dassie well inside it or leaves it on the sky; no outline just touches its edge. On a phone, a scene gets a composition of its own, never the wide one shrunk.

Framing the illustrations
Always grounded. A dassie stands on a horizon or a stone, with its shadow: Shade at 24%, darkening whatever ground it falls on. It never floats in a box.

7Voice

Talk like a neighbor at sunrise.

Calm, plain and warm. We take money seriously, so we say exactly what is true; we enjoy the work, so we don't sound like a bank.

Out, not up

Describe the network as people next to each other, never as a power above them.

We sayYour node peers with a few neighbors. Together you're the network.
We don't sayJoin the most powerful payment infrastructure ever built.

It's early, and that's fine

Say how far along we are. The sun is coming up; it isn't noon.

We sayDassie is early: few users and few ledgers so far, with more every day.
We don't sayThe future of finance is here. Start earning today.

Trust is earned sideways

Explain how standing grows between peers instead of asking anyone to trust us.

We sayYour node sends more payments through the peers that earn it the most.
We don't sayTrustless, unstoppable, bank-grade security.

Plain words, warm tone

Short sentences, everyday verbs. A little sun, never a joke at the user's expense.

We saySent. Kai has 4.20 DSUSD. Your node settles with boot2 later today.
We don't sayTransaction broadcast to the decentralized settlement layer. 🚀

9Care

Why it works, and where it needs care.

Why it works

  • It says the idea in one picture. Solid rocks, nimble dassies: the ledgers hold still, the nodes move, and nobody needs a tower. The mark says it before anyone reads a word.
  • Warm without being cute. Sandstone, sunrise and a soft serif feel like a good co-op or outdoors brand: friendly, calm, and still credible with money.
  • Honest about being early. Sunrise carries “it's early” as optimism instead of an apology, and it can grow into midday later.

Watch out for

  • Beige drift. Sand on sand can go flat and sleepy. Keep the ink horizon, the backlit rock, real Ember buttons and the violet cube in every layout.
  • Wellness and travel look-alikes. Sunrises and soft serifs are common there. The backlit rock, the kopje line and the dassies are what make it ours; never ship only a gradient.
  • Wide on narrow screens. A horizontal system has to fold on phones: kopje lines shorten, a scene gets a composition of its own, and below 48 px the mark switches to its Silhouette.
